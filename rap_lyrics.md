## **PRODUCTION NOTES**

- All beats are designed to be around 140-150 BPM for that hardcore futuristic feel
- Each song incorporates scene-specific sound effects and atmosphere
- Lyrics blend cyberpunk terminology with classic rap bravado
- Flow patterns emphasize the transformation from contained experiment to liberated artist
- Wordplay focuses on digital/technological themes mixed with urban slang
- Each track represents a chapter in Squeaky G's evolution

**Musical Style:** Hardcore cyberpunk rap with glitch effects, heavy bass, and electronic elements
**Vocal Delivery:** Aggressive yet articulate, with sections of robotic vocoder effects
**Themes:** Freedom, creativity, consciousness, rebellion through art, human-AI collaboration
